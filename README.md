# The Cleaner — ذا كلينر (mock prototype)

Interactive UX prototype (v1.4) with **mock data only**. Bookings, driver collection, bills and payments are simulated: nothing is charged, no card details are collected, and nothing is connected to CleanCloud, Wafeq or a payment gateway.

Prices come from The Cleaner price list (SAR). VAT treatment is pending confirmation.
